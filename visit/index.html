Getting here

Well connected

Central Park Mall sits at the hub of Sydney's trains, buses, light rail and cycleways.

Walking from Central Station

  1. Leave Central Station via the South Concourse, Exit 7, Railway Square.
  2. Head to George Street and turn toward Broadway.
  3. Walk a few minutes along Broadway. Central Park Mall is at 28 Broadway, opposite UTS.

About 5 minutes. Redfern Station is about 10 minutes' walk.

Everyone welcome
