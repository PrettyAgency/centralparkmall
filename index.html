The precinct

Shop under the gardens

Central Park Mall sits in the base of One Central Park, beneath Jean Nouvel's towers and Patrick Blanc's vertical gardens, with Chippendale Green right outside.

It's a place to stop between classes, catch a film after work, or spend a whole Saturday.
